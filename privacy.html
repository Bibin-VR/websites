Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

What we collect

Your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and the business details and files you send us for your website. From Razorpay, we receive payment confirmation details: amount, date and payment ID.

What we never receive

Your card number, UPI PIN or bank login. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

How we use it

Only to build, deliver and support your website, and to send you receipts and service messages about your order.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. Business details you ask us to publish, such as your address and phone number, will appear on your public website. Sites are hosted on GitHub Pages and payments are processed by Razorpay; each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and third parties

This site doesn't set cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps, which receive your IP address when they load.

How long we keep it

We keep your project files and messages for as long as needed to support your site, and delete them when you ask, except records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email us.
